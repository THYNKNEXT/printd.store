Our story

A new B2B brand, built on complementary experience.

The Printd Store is jointly created by MANIFOLD GLOBAL ENTERPRISES and SIVA SAI ENTERPRISES to give organisations a clearer, more considered way to source merchandise, printing and branded materials.

Why we came together

Good merchandise needs both a strong idea and a dependable path to execution.

The Printd Store brings together the experience of two businesses with different but connected strengths. Our purpose is simple: make branded merchandise easier to plan, clearer to source and more useful to the organisations that invest in it.

MANIFOLD GLOBAL ENTERPRISES

Brand, digital and customer experience

MANIFOLD contributes the customer-facing foundation: brand thinking, digital presence, website experience, marketing direction and the systems needed to make enquiries easier to manage.

SIVA SAI ENTERPRISES

Production and procurement experience

SIVA SAI contributes the existing portfolio experience behind customized gifts, corporate merchandise, printing, stationery, branding materials and promotional products.

What this means for clients

One brand, designed to make the process feel joined up.

You bring the need. The Printd Store helps convert it into a suitable physical expression of your brand - whether that is a corporate gift, team apparel, printed office material or event merchandise.

Business-first recommendations

We begin with the purpose of the project, not a one-size-fits-all product catalogue.

Clearer coordination

Brand discussion, product planning and execution are considered as one client experience.

Grounded in working capability

The brand is built from existing operational and portfolio experience, not only an online storefront.

Built with care

Trust is earned project by project.

The legal operating, invoicing and procurement arrangement for a particular project will always be confirmed in the formal quotation and documentation. This website introduces The Printd Store as the client-facing B2B brand; it does not replace project-specific terms.
